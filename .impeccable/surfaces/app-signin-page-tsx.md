# /signin landing page

Scope: Persuade. Visitors are pharmacists and clinic staff, new or returning, on a phone at the shelf or at a desk. Job: understand MedMinder in seconds, then sign in or open an account. Proof: the 32s demo film, the live sample shelf, three real features. Constraint: landing uses the app's light canvas and tokens (serif display, stamps); no invented claims.

Answers: both CTAs at equal weight; the form lives in its own section below the hero; the demo video is the hero visual; proof follows in its own section.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One section, one job. The hero only says what this is and offers two equal doors; it refuses the form-beside-the-headline crowd. Proof and access each get a full section of their own.
OWN-WORLD: The app's own light canvas (#FAFAFA), white bordered cards with shadow-xs, serif display, muted-foreground supporting text, rectangular 8-10px controls, hairline dividers, stamps as the only colour besides violet. Violet Button primary and outline secondary of identical size.
STORY: The visitor sees the headline and the film running, believes the tool tracks expiry for them, and takes either door. Scrolling confirms it with the shelf whose stamps change, then the form is waiting.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, wordmark above a two-line serif headline, one sentence, then two same-size buttons (Sign in, Open an account). Right, the autoplaying demo film at about 55% of the width. On a phone the same order stacks, left aligned, buttons full width.
FORM: Asymmetric split hero, then a reversed split proof section, then a single centred access card. Chosen by the user's answers; no concept roll, seed key: none (precisely specified request).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
